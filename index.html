Kids Math Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Kids Math is a children’s arithmetic practice app provided by Vilosh Tech. This policy describes the current version of the app.

Information collection and sharing

Kids Math does not collect or transmit personal information, practice answers, scores, or device identifiers to Vilosh Tech or third parties. No account or sign-in is required. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

Practice data

Questions, selected answers, scores, and streaks are held in memory during use. They are not saved as a practice history or sent to a server. Starting a new practice session resets the session results. Android may keep a session in memory while the app is in the background; ending its process clears that session data.

Ads and analytics

This version contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Math practice works offline.

Children’s privacy

We do not ask children to provide personal information. A parent or guardian can contact us with privacy questions using the support email below.

Contacting us

If you choose to email Vilosh Tech, we receive your email address and any information you include through our email provider. We use that information to respond to your request. Please do not include a child’s personal information. You can request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable legal obligations. Email is handled outside the app by your chosen email service and our email provider.

Support email: vilosh.tech@gmail.com

Policy updates

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. Review the policy again after app updates, especially if new online features are introduced.
